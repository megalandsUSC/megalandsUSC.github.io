Citizen Science

DISCOVER includes a citizen science component hosted on Zooniverse, the world's largest people-powered research platform, with over 2 million registered volunteers.

Help us discover hidden burial mounds!

Anyone — with no prior knowledge of archaeology — can contribute to the project by examining LiDAR images of the Galician landscape and flagging shapes that might correspond to prehistoric burial mounds.

🔭 Go to DISCOVER on Zooniverse

How it works

1
Free registration

Create a free Zooniverse account or participate anonymously

2
Examine LiDAR images

The tool presents analytical images derived from LiDAR data of the Galician landscape

3
Search and classify

Identify rounded raised shapes (burial mounds) or central depressions (looting pits). Not all images contain archaeological features

4
Feed the AI

Your classifications are used to retrain the automatic detection algorithm, improving its accuracy

What to look for

Galician megalithic monuments appear in two main forms:

Burial mounds (mámoas/túmulos): Small human-made elevations of the ground, typically 1–2 metres high, built as funerary monuments. Some show a central depression or looting pit caused by illegal excavations in the past.

Dolmens: Some monuments have lost their earth mass and only the stone chamber survives. They vary in type: simple polygonal chambers, short-corridor dolmens, long-corridor dolmens, or small rectangular cists.

Remember: not all images you review will contain archaeological sites. Part of the task is precisely to distinguish natural landforms from human-made structures.

Why does your participation matter?

The Galician landscape is vast. Applying the AI algorithm and validating its results through expert-only review would take years. With citizen participation, we can:

  • Scale validation to large areas of territory in a short time
  • Improve model training with thousands of human-generated labels
  • Detect errors in existing heritage catalogues
  • Raise public awareness of the richness of Galicia's megalithic heritage

“Many prehistoric monuments are still hidden in today's landscapes. With your help, we can reveal them and better understand how people shaped the land thousands of years ago.”

Miguel Carrero-Pazos

Background and inspiration

DISCOVER builds on previous experiences in European archaeology, in particular the Heritage Quest project by Leiden University (Netherlands), which demonstrated the effectiveness of citizen science for LiDAR-based archaeological feature detection.
