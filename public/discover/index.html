Automatic Detection of Tumular and Megalithic Monuments using LiDAR and Artificial Intelligence

The DISCOVER project integrates airborne LiDAR data, High-Performance Computing (HPC) and artificial intelligence to improve the catalogue of megalithic heritage in Galicia (NW Spain). Funded by Fondos Impulso – USC. CISPAC, 2025–2026.

Galicia holds one of the largest concentrations of tumular and megalithic monuments on the European Atlantic façade. Yet the available archaeological catalogues suffer from reliability problems: duplicate records, coordinate errors, and large areas of territory still unsurveyed. DISCOVER aims to make a methodological leap by combining LiDAR technology, high-performance computing and machine learning to systematically improve that catalogue.
